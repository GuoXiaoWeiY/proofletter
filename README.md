## What is ProofLetter?

ProofLetter is an AI-powered document generator for freelancers, independent contractors, and remote workers who need to prove their income. Traditional employment letters do not exist for self-employed people - banks, landlords, and visa officers still ask for them.

ProofLetter fills the gap: users answer a short guided form about their work, income, and clients, and the platform produces a clean, professional proof of income letter in minutes.

## Use cases

- Renting an apartment
- Opening a bank account
- Applying for a loan
- Visa and residency applications
